Who we are

A spotless finish,

Once the trades are off site, we take care of the detail that makes a project truly ready. We remove every trace of the build: fine construction dust, render splatter, paint, silicone, stickers and marks. FAQs

Common questions

What's included in a builders clean?+

Dust removed from every surface, including walls, skirting, doors, light switches and fittings. Paint, silicone, render splatter and stickers removed. Windows, frames and tracks cleaned inside and out. Cupboards and drawers cleaned inside and out. Kitchen and bathrooms detailed, and floors vacuumed and mopped.

What's the difference between a rough clean and a sparkle clean?+

A rough clean happens during construction, after the main trades. Rubbish out, floors done and heavy dust off, so the next trade can start. A sparkle clean is the final detailed clean once everyone is off site, ready for inspection and keys.

How do you price a job?+

Every job gets a fixed, itemised quote based on the size of the home, the stage of the build and what's included. Send us the address and a few photos and we'll usually quote within one business day.

Do you work through the wet season?+

Yes. We book around your program and the weather, and plan external work like windows and house washing for dry days. Homes that have been closed up get aired out and wiped down so they're fresh for handover.

Are you insured?+

Yes, we're fully insured, and we're happy to send through our certificate of currency for your site records.

How far ahead should I book?+

One to two weeks' notice is ideal, especially before Christmas and end of financial year. Get in touch as soon as you have a handover date and we'll pencil you in.
